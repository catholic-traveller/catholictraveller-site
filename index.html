Covers Catholic churches across the UK. Spotted a missing church or a wrong Mass time? You can fix it in the app: use Suggest a missing church in the ☰ menu, or the edit button on any church's page.

How Mass times are kept up to date

Each church's times are checked every week against its parish website or newsletter, where the parish has one. When someone suggests a change, an automated checker compares it with the parish's own website, and anything it can't confirm is reviewed by a person.

Mass times can still change at short notice, so it's always worth a quick check with the parish before you travel.
