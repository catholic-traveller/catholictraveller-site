Please check Mass times before you travel

Catholic Traveller gathers Mass times from parish websites, diocesan directories and reports from other travellers, and we work hard to keep them accurate. But parishes change their Mass times — for holidays, feast days, clergy availability and building works — often at short notice, and not every change reaches us straight away.

Always confirm the time with the parish before you set off, for example by checking its website or newsletter, or by phoning the parish office. Where the app links to a parish's website, that website is the authoritative source.

Travel times and routes

Travel times, distances and routes are estimates calculated by computer and do not account for live traffic, road closures, weather or parking. Allow extra time for your journey. When road routing is unavailable, the app says so and shows approximate straight-line distances instead.

Parking and accessibility

Parking, motorway-proximity and accessibility information is provided as a helpful guide only. It may be incomplete or out of date, so please check with the parish if it matters for your visit.
